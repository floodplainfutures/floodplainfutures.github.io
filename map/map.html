About this map: Real geographic data displayed using GeoJSON layers. Click on any polygon or trail to see detailed information. Toggle layers to explore different aspects of the Yolo Basin ecosystem.

Base map: © OpenStreetMap contributors. GeoJSON layers: Yolo Basin boundaries, Yolo Bypass Wildlife Area, hiking trails, Nigiri Project area, & Pacific Flyway migration corridor. Earth Engine layers: Google Earth Engine satellite imagery and water data. Bird observations: eBird (2004–2026).

Note: GeoJSON polygon coordinates are approximate representations for demonstration. For official boundary data, please consult California Department of Water Resources and California Department of Fish and Wildlife.
