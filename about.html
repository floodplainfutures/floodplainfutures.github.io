the yolo basin project

An interactive thesis exploring seasonal flooding and bird migrations at the Yolo Basin. Created by zee to make ecology approachable, playful, and visually engaging.

my thesis

Investigates how seasonal flooding intersects with bird migrations, using interactive visualizations to make science approachable for locals and researchers.

Combines eBird and USGS data with web-based design, turning ecological patterns into something minimal, playful, and story-driven.

about zee

Designer & researcher passionate about interactive data, minimal aesthetics, and storytelling. This project combines my love for ecology, typography, and UX design.

the yolo basin

A dynamic floodplain in Northern California, crucial for birds, flood control, and local ecology. Seasonal inundation creates a living laboratory to observe nature's patterns.

Inspires the visual language and interactivity of this project.
