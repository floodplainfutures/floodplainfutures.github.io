the pattern

The curves move together. When water rises (blue), birds arrive (pink). When water recedes, birds follow. The space between shows their connection.

what you're seeing

22 years of data (2004-2026):
→ Water: USGS flood sensors
→ Birds: eBird citizen science
Both normalized to show relationship clearly

why it matters

The Yolo Bypass is flood control infrastructure. But when it floods, it accidentally becomes a massive bird habitat—recreating the seasonal wetlands that used to cover the Central Valley.
