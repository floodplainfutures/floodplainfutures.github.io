the pattern

The year spins around the circle. Winter months (top) see flooding and massive bird arrivals. Summer (bottom) is dry with few birds. The cycle repeats.

why it matters

The Yolo Bypass is flood control infrastructure. But when it floods, it accidentally becomes a massive bird habitat—recreating the seasonal wetlands that used to cover the Central Valley.
